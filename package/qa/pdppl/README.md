# Qatar Personal Data Privacy Protection Law (PDPPL)

Qatar's Law No. 13 of 2016 on the protection of personal data privacy (PDPPL) governs the
processing of personal data in Qatar. It is overseen by the National Cyber Security Agency
(NCSA).

## What it includes

- **Individual rights:** Rights of individuals over their personal data.
- **Controller and processor obligations:** Requirements for lawful processing and security.
- **Special categories:** Additional rules for personal data of a special nature.
- **Direct marketing and children:** Rules on electronic direct marketing and websites aimed at
  children.
