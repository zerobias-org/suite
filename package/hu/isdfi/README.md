# Act CXII of 2011 on Informational Self-Determination and Freedom of Information

Act CXII of 2011 is Hungary's law on data protection and on access to public information. It
also sets up the Hungarian National Authority for Data Protection and Freedom of Information
(NAIH).

## What it includes

- **Data protection:** Rules on processing personal data that complement the EU GDPR.
- **Freedom of information:** Rules on access to data of public interest.
- **Supervisory authority:** Organisation, powers and procedures of the NAIH.
