# French Data Protection Act (Law No. 78-17)

Law No. 78-17 of 6 January 1978 on information technology, data files and civil liberties
(Loi Informatique et Libertés) is France's data protection law. It has been amended several
times, including to complement the EU GDPR. The CNIL is the authority it set up.

## What it includes

- **The CNIL:** Status, powers and duties of the Commission nationale de l'informatique et
  des libertés.
- **GDPR complements:** National rules where the GDPR allows member states to legislate.
- **Law enforcement processing:** Rules that implement Directive (EU) 2016/680.
- **Enforcement:** Sanctions and procedures.
