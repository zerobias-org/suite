# COSO Enterprise Risk Management Framework (2017)

"Enterprise Risk Management: Integrating with Strategy and Performance" is the 2017 enterprise risk
management framework of the Committee of Sponsoring Organizations of the Treadway Commission
(COSO). It links risk management to strategy setting and performance.

## What it includes

- **Governance and Culture:** Board oversight, operating structures and culture.
- **Strategy and Objective-Setting:** Risk appetite and its link to strategy and objectives.
- **Performance:** Identifying, assessing, prioritizing and responding to risks.
- **Review and Revision:** Reviewing risk performance and adapting to change.
- **Information, Communication, and Reporting:** Sharing risk information across the organization.
