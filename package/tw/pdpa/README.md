# Taiwan Personal Data Protection Act

The Personal Data Protection Act is Taiwan's general data protection law. It regulates the collection,
processing and use of personal data by government agencies and non-government entities.

## What it includes

- **Collection, processing and use:** conditions under which personal data may be handled
- **Sensitive data:** restrictions on medical, genetic, criminal and similar records
- **Data subject rights:** inquiry, review, copies, correction, cessation and deletion
- **Security and notification:** security measures and notification of data breaches
- **Penalties:** administrative, civil and criminal liability for violations
