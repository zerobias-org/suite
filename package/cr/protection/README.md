# Law No. 8968 on the Protection of the Person in the Processing of Personal Data (Costa Rica)

Law No. 8968 is Costa Rica's personal data protection law. It protects the right to informational
self-determination and is enforced by the Agency for the Protection of Inhabitants' Data (PRODHAB).

## What it includes

- **Principles:** Informational self-determination, informed consent and data quality.
- **Data subject rights:** Rights of access, rectification and deletion.
- **Sensitive data:** Special protection for sensitive personal data.
- **Security and confidentiality:** Security obligations and a duty of confidentiality for those
  who handle personal data.
- **PRODHAB:** The supervisory agency, its powers and sanctions.
