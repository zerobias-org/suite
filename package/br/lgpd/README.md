# General Data Protection Law (LGPD)

The Lei Geral de Proteção de Dados (LGPD, Law No. 13,709/2018) is Brazil's general data protection
law. It applies to the processing of personal data in Brazil and is enforced by the National Data
Protection Authority (ANPD).

## What it includes

- **Principles and legal bases:** Principles for processing and the legal bases that permit it.
- **Sensitive and children's data:** Additional rules for sensitive data and data of children and
  adolescents.
- **Data subject rights:** Rights such as access, correction, deletion and portability.
- **International transfers:** Conditions for transferring personal data abroad.
- **Governance and enforcement:** Data protection officer, security and incident reporting, and
  ANPD sanctions.
