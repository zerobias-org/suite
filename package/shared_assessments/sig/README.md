# Shared Assessments Standardized Information Gathering (SIG) Questionnaire

The SIG questionnaire is published by Shared Assessments for third-party risk management. Organizations
use it to collect information about a vendor's security, privacy and resilience controls in a
standard format.

## What it includes

- **SIG Core:** the full questionnaire for in-depth assessment of higher-risk third parties
- **SIG Lite:** a shorter questionnaire for initial or lower-risk assessments
- **Risk domains:** questions grouped by topic, such as security, privacy and business resilience
- **Content mappings:** references from SIG questions to common standards and regulations
