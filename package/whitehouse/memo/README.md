# Memoranda

This suite covers memoranda that give direction to U.S. federal agencies, published by the Office of
Management and Budget (OMB) in the Executive Office of the President. OMB memoranda set government-wide
policy on topics such as information security, privacy and IT management.

## What it includes

- **OMB memoranda:** Numbered memoranda (M-series) that direct federal agencies.
- **Topics:** Policy and implementation guidance on cybersecurity, privacy, IT and agency
  management.
