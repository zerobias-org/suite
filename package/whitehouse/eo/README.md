# Executive Orders

Executive orders are directives the President of the United States issues to manage the operations
of the federal government. They are published in the Federal Register. This suite groups executive
orders, for example those that set federal cybersecurity or privacy requirements.

## What it includes

- **Executive orders:** Signed presidential directives to federal agencies, each with its own number.
- **Publication:** Text of each order as published in the Federal Register.
- **Amendments and revocations:** Later orders that amend or revoke earlier ones.

## Official resources

- Executive orders (Federal Register): https://www.federalregister.gov/presidential-documents/executive-orders
- Executive orders (The White House): https://www.whitehouse.gov/presidential-actions/executive-orders/
