# Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA)

The Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 (POPIA) is South Africa's data protection law.
It regulates the processing of personal information by public and private bodies and established
the Information Regulator, which enforces it.

## What it includes

- **Conditions for lawful processing:** Conditions such as accountability, processing limitation,
  purpose specification, information quality, openness, security safeguards and data subject
  participation.
- **Data subject rights:** Rights to be informed, to access, correct and delete personal
  information, and to object to processing.
- **Special categories:** Rules for special personal information and information about children.
- **Direct marketing and transfers:** Limits on direct marketing and on transfers of personal
  information outside South Africa.
- **Information Regulator:** Powers of the Information Regulator and enforcement.
