# Colorado Privacy Act (CPA)

The Colorado Privacy Act is a Colorado state consumer data privacy law, enacted as Senate Bill
21-190. It is enforced by the Colorado Attorney General and district attorneys, and the Attorney
General issues rules under it.

## What it includes

- **Consumer rights:** Rights to access, correct, delete and port personal data, and to opt out of
  targeted advertising, sale and certain profiling.
- **Controller duties:** Transparency, purpose specification, data minimization, security and
  consent for processing sensitive data.
- **Data protection assessments:** Assessments for processing that presents a heightened risk of
  harm.
- **Universal opt-out:** Recognition of universal opt-out mechanisms.
