# Uruguay Law No. 18.331 - Personal Data Protection and Habeas Data

Law No. 18.331 is Uruguay's personal data protection law. It also sets up the habeas data action,
which lets people go to court over their personal data. It is overseen by the Regulatory and
Control Unit for Personal Data (URCDP).

## What it includes

- **Principles:** Principles for processing personal data, such as legality, accuracy, purpose,
  prior informed consent and data security.
- **Data subject rights:** Rights of access, rectification, update, inclusion and deletion.
- **Database obligations:** Rules for those who hold databases, including registration and
  international data transfers.
- **Supervisory authority:** Creation and powers of the URCDP.
- **Habeas data action:** A judicial action to protect personal data.
