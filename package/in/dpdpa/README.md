# Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023

The Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (Act No. 22 of 2023) is India's law on the processing of
digital personal data. It is administered by the Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology
(MeitY) and enforced by the Data Protection Board of India.

## What it includes

- **Obligations of Data Fiduciaries:** Lawful grounds, notice and consent, security safeguards, breach
  notification and data retention.
- **Rights and duties of Data Principals:** Access, correction and erasure, grievance redressal and
  nomination.
- **Special provisions:** Processing of children's data, Significant Data Fiduciaries and transfers
  outside India.
- **Data Protection Board of India:** The Board's powers, procedure, appeals and penalties.
