# Information Technology Rules (Privacy Rules)

The Information Technology (Reasonable security practices and procedures and sensitive personal data or
information) Rules, 2011 are issued by the Government of India under the Information Technology Act,
2000. They set out how organizations must collect, handle and protect personal information.

## What it includes

- **Sensitive personal data or information:** Defines covered categories such as passwords, financial,
  health and biometric information.
- **Privacy policy:** Requires a published policy on what is collected, why, and how it is protected.
- **Collection, disclosure and transfer:** Consent, purpose limitation, a Grievance Officer, and limits
  on disclosure and transfer to third parties.
- **Reasonable security practices:** A documented information security program, with ISO/IEC 27001 named
  as an example standard, subject to audit.
