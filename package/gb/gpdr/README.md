# UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR)

The UK GDPR is the version of the EU GDPR retained in United Kingdom law. It is read
together with the Data Protection Act 2018 and enforced by the Information Commissioner's
Office (ICO).

## What it includes

- **Principles and lawful bases:** Core data protection principles and legal grounds for
  processing.
- **Individual rights:** Rights such as access, rectification, erasure and objection.
- **Controller and processor duties:** Accountability, security and breach notification.
- **International transfers:** Rules for transferring personal data outside the UK.
