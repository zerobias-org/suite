# Norway Personal Data Act

The Norwegian Personal Data Act (Act of 15 June 2018 No. 38, personopplysningsloven) incorporates
the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) into Norwegian law and adds national rules. It is
supervised by the Norwegian Data Protection Authority (Datatilsynet).

## What it includes

- **GDPR as Norwegian law:** The GDPR applies in Norway through the Act.
- **National rules:** Supplementary provisions where the GDPR leaves room for national rules.
- **Supervision and enforcement:** The role of Datatilsynet and the Privacy Appeals Board.
