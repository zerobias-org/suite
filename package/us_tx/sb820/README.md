# Texas SB 820 (2019) - School District Cybersecurity

Senate Bill 820 was enacted by the Texas Legislature in its 86th regular session (2019). It requires
Texas school districts to adopt a cybersecurity policy, by adding Section 11.175 to the Texas
Education Code.

## What it includes

- **Cybersecurity policy:** Each school district must adopt a cybersecurity policy to protect its
  information and systems.
- **Cybersecurity coordinator:** Each district must designate a cybersecurity coordinator.
- **Breach reporting:** Reporting of breaches of system security involving student information.
