# Texas Data Privacy and Security Act (HB 4)

The Texas Data Privacy and Security Act is a Texas state consumer data privacy law, enacted as House
Bill 4 in the 88th regular session of the Texas Legislature. It is enforced by the Texas Attorney
General.

## What it includes

- **Consumer rights:** Rights to access, correct, delete and port personal data, and to opt out of
  targeted advertising, sale and certain profiling.
- **Controller duties:** Privacy notices, data minimization, security and consent for processing
  sensitive data.
- **Data protection assessments:** Assessments for certain types of processing.
- **Enforcement:** Enforcement by the Attorney General.
