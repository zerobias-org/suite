# Texas DIR Security Control Standards Catalog

The Security Control Standards Catalog is published by the Texas Department of Information
Resources (DIR). It gives Texas state agencies and institutions of higher education the minimum
required information security controls, in a format aligned with NIST SP 800-53 Revision 5, and
supports 1 Texas Administrative Code Chapter 202.

## What it includes

- **Control families:** Access control, awareness and training, audit, assessment and monitoring,
  configuration management and contingency planning.
- **More control families:** Identification and authentication, incident response, maintenance,
  media protection, physical and environmental protection and planning.
- **Program and risk controls:** Program management, personnel security, risk assessment, system
  and services acquisition and supply chain risk management.
- **System protection:** System and communications protection and system and information integrity.
