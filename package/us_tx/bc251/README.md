# Texas Business and Commerce Code Chapter 521

Chapter 521 of the Texas Business and Commerce Code, Unauthorized Use of Identifying Information,
contains the Identity Theft Enforcement and Protection Act. It is a Texas state law enforced by the
Texas Attorney General.

## What it includes

- **Identity theft:** Prohibits obtaining, possessing or using another person's identifying
  information without consent.
- **Protecting sensitive personal information:** Requires businesses to use reasonable procedures to
  protect sensitive personal information and to dispose of records securely.
- **Breach notification:** Notice to affected individuals, and to the Attorney General for larger
  breaches, after a breach of system security.
- **Remedies:** Civil penalties and other remedies the Attorney General can seek.
