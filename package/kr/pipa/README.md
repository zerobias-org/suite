# Personal Information Protection Act

The Personal Information Protection Act (PIPA) is South Korea's general data protection law. It is
administered by the Personal Information Protection Commission (PIPC).

## What it includes

- **Processing principles:** Purpose limitation, minimum collection and consent requirements.
- **Sensitive and unique identifying information:** Extra limits on processing these categories.
- **Rights of data subjects:** Access, correction, deletion and suspension of processing.
- **Safeguards and breach notification:** Security measures and notification of personal information
  leaks.
- **Enforcement:** The PIPC's powers, dispute mediation and penalties.
