# Statutory Law 1581 of 2012 (Colombia)

Statutory Law 1581 of 2012 is Colombia's general personal data protection law. It sets the rules
for processing personal data in databases and is supervised by the Superintendence of Industry and
Commerce (SIC).

## What it includes

- **Principles:** Legality, purpose, freedom (consent), accuracy, transparency, security and
  confidentiality.
- **Special categories:** Rules for sensitive data and data of children and adolescents.
- **Data subject rights:** Rights to know, update, correct and request deletion of personal data.
- **Controller and processor duties:** Obligations for those who handle personal data.
- **International transfers and supervision:** Transfer restrictions and the SIC's oversight role.
