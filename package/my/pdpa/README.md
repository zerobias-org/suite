# Personal Data Protection Act of 2010

The Personal Data Protection Act 2010 (Act 709) is Malaysia's law on the processing of personal data in
commercial transactions. It is administered by the Personal Data Protection Commissioner.

## What it includes

- **Personal Data Protection Principles:** General, Notice and Choice, Disclosure, Security, Retention,
  Data Integrity and Access principles.
- **Rights of data subjects:** Access, correction and withdrawal of consent.
- **Registration:** Registration of data users in specified classes.
- **Enforcement:** The Commissioner's powers and offences under the Act.
