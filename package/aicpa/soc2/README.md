# SOC 2

SOC 2 is a report on controls at a service organization relevant to security, availability,
processing integrity, confidentiality and privacy. The SOC 2 examination and the Trust Services
Criteria it uses are published by the AICPA.

## What it includes

- **Trust Services Criteria:** The criteria for security (common criteria), availability,
  processing integrity, confidentiality and privacy.
- **Type 1 report:** An opinion on the design of controls at a point in time.
- **Type 2 report:** An opinion on the design and operating effectiveness of controls over a period.
- **System description:** Management's description of the service organization's system.
