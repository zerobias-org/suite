# General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR)

The GDPR (Regulation (EU) 2016/679) is European Union law on the protection of personal data.
It applies to organizations that process the personal data of people in the EU.

## What it includes

- **Principles and lawful bases:** Core data protection principles and the legal grounds for
  processing.
- **Data subject rights:** Rights such as access, rectification, erasure and objection.
- **Controller and processor duties:** Accountability, security, breach notification, data
  protection impact assessments and data protection officers.
- **International transfers:** Rules for transferring personal data outside the EU.
- **Supervision and enforcement:** Supervisory authorities, the European Data Protection
  Board, remedies and fines.
