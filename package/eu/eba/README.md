# EBA Guidelines on ICT and security risk management

The EBA Guidelines on ICT and security risk management (EBA/GL/2019/04) set out how
financial institutions should manage ICT and security risks. They are issued by the European
Banking Authority (EBA).

## What it includes

- **Governance and strategy:** Roles, responsibilities and ICT strategy.
- **Risk management framework:** Identifying, assessing and mitigating ICT and security risk.
- **Information security:** Policy, controls, monitoring and testing.
- **ICT operations and change:** ICT operations, project and change management.
- **Business continuity:** Continuity planning, testing and crisis communication.
