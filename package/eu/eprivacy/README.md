# ePrivacy Directive (Directive 2002/58/EC)

The ePrivacy Directive is European Union law on privacy and electronic communications. It
complements EU data protection law for the electronic communications sector.

## What it includes

- **Confidentiality of communications:** Protection of communications and related data.
- **Traffic and location data:** Rules on processing and retaining such data.
- **Terminal equipment:** Consent rules for storing or accessing information on user
  devices, such as cookies.
- **Unsolicited communications:** Rules on direct marketing by email, SMS and calls.
- **Security and breaches:** Security duties and breach notification for providers.
