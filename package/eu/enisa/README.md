# NIS2 Directive (Directive (EU) 2022/2555)

The NIS2 Directive is European Union law that sets a common level of cybersecurity for
essential and important entities across critical sectors. EU member states transpose it into
national law, and ENISA, the EU Agency for Cybersecurity, supports its implementation.

## What it includes

- **Scope:** Essential and important entities in the sectors listed in its annexes.
- **Risk-management measures:** Cybersecurity measures entities must take, with
  accountability for management bodies.
- **Incident reporting:** Reporting of significant incidents to CSIRTs or competent
  authorities.
- **National and EU cooperation:** National strategies and authorities, CSIRTs, the
  Cooperation Group and the CSIRTs network.
- **Supervision and enforcement:** Supervisory powers and penalties.
