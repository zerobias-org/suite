# Second Payment Services Directive (PSD2)

PSD2 (Directive (EU) 2015/2366) is European Union law on payment services in the internal
market. The European Banking Authority (EBA) develops technical standards and guidelines
under it.

## What it includes

- **Payment institutions:** Authorisation and supervision of payment service providers.
- **Third-party providers:** Access to payment accounts for payment initiation and account
  information services.
- **Strong customer authentication:** Authentication and secure communication requirements,
  set out in EBA standards.
- **Transparency and user rights:** Information requirements and rights and obligations for
  payment services.
- **Security and incidents:** Operational and security risk management and incident
  reporting.
