# EU Cyber Resilience Act (Regulation (EU) 2024/2847)

The Cyber Resilience Act is a European Union regulation that sets cybersecurity requirements
for products with digital elements, such as hardware and software, placed on the EU market.

## What it includes

- **Essential requirements:** Security requirements for product properties and for
  vulnerability handling.
- **Economic operator obligations:** Duties for manufacturers, importers and distributors.
- **Reporting:** Reporting of actively exploited vulnerabilities and severe incidents.
- **Conformity assessment:** Assessment procedures and CE marking, with stricter rules for
  important and critical products.
