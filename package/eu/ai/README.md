# EU Artificial Intelligence Act (Regulation (EU) 2024/1689)

The AI Act is a European Union regulation that sets harmonised rules for artificial
intelligence. It takes a risk-based approach to AI systems placed on the EU market.

## What it includes

- **Prohibited practices:** AI practices that are banned in the EU.
- **High-risk AI systems:** Requirements for high-risk systems and obligations for their
  providers and deployers.
- **Transparency obligations:** Disclosure rules for certain AI systems, such as chatbots and
  generated content.
- **General-purpose AI models:** Obligations for providers of general-purpose AI models.
- **Governance and enforcement:** The AI Office, national authorities and penalties.
