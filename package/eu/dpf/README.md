# EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework

The EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework is a mechanism for transferring personal data from the
European Union to certified organizations in the United States. It rests on a European
Commission adequacy decision and is administered in the U.S. by the Department of Commerce.

## What it includes

- **Principles:** The Data Privacy Framework Principles that participating organizations
  must follow.
- **Self-certification:** The process for U.S. organizations to join and stay on the list.
- **Participant list:** A public list of certified organizations.
- **Redress:** Ways for individuals to raise complaints, including independent recourse.
