# EU Digital Operational Resilience Act (DORA)

DORA (Regulation (EU) 2022/2554) is a European Union regulation on the digital operational
resilience of the financial sector. It applies to financial entities and to their ICT
third-party service providers.

## What it includes

- **ICT risk management:** Governance and a framework for managing ICT risk.
- **ICT incident reporting:** Classification and reporting of major ICT-related incidents.
- **Resilience testing:** Digital operational resilience testing, including threat-led
  penetration testing.
- **Third-party risk:** Management of ICT third-party risk and an oversight framework for
  critical ICT third-party providers.
- **Information sharing:** Arrangements for sharing cyber threat information.
