# California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA)

The California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA) amends and expands the California Consumer Privacy Act
(CCPA). It created the California Privacy Protection Agency (CPPA), which issues regulations under
the law. This suite tracks the November 2022 version of the CCPA regulations text.

## What it includes

- **Consumer rights:** Rights to know, delete, correct and opt out of the sale or sharing of personal
  information.
- **Sensitive personal information:** A right to limit the use and disclosure of sensitive personal
  information.
- **Business obligations:** Notices, contracts with service providers and contractors, and
  reasonable security.
- **Regulations and enforcement:** CPPA rulemaking and enforcement alongside the California Attorney
  General.
