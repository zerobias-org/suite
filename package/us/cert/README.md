# CERT Resilience Management Model (CERT-RMM)

The CERT Resilience Management Model (CERT-RMM) is a capability model for managing operational resilience,
developed by the CERT Division of the Software Engineering Institute (SEI) at Carnegie Mellon University.

## What it includes

- **Process areas:** practices across enterprise management, engineering, operations and process
  management
- **Capability levels:** levels describing how well each process area is institutionalized
- **Asset focus:** protection and sustainment of people, information, technology and facilities
