# Farm Credit Administration (FCA) Cyber Risk Management

The Farm Credit Administration's cyber risk management rule requires Farm Credit System institutions to
maintain a cyber risk management program. It is codified at 12 CFR Part 609, Subpart J.

## What it includes

- **Cyber risk management program:** a written, board-approved program for managing cyber risk
- **Risk assessment and controls:** identifying cyber risks and implementing safeguards
- **Third-party risk:** oversight of service providers with access to institution systems
- **Training and reporting:** staff training and regular reporting to the board
