# Cybersecurity Capability Maturity Model (C2M2) v2.1

The Cybersecurity Capability Maturity Model (C2M2) is a free tool from the U.S. Department of Energy that
helps organizations evaluate and improve their cybersecurity capabilities. It was developed with energy
sector organizations but can be used by any organization.

## What it includes

- **Domains:** groups of practices such as asset, threat, risk, access, situation, response and
  workforce management
- **Maturity indicator levels:** levels that measure how complete and institutionalized practices
  are
- **Self-evaluation tool:** a facilitated or self-run assessment of the model's practices
