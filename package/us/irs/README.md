# Internal Revenue Service (IRS) Publication 1075

IRS Publication 1075, Tax Information Security Guidelines for Federal, State and Local Agencies, is
published by the U.S. Internal Revenue Service. It sets the safeguarding requirements agencies and
their contractors must meet to receive and handle federal tax information (FTI).

## What it includes

- **Safeguarding requirements:** Record keeping, secure storage, restricted access and disposal of
  FTI.
- **Reporting:** Safeguard Security Reports and other reports agencies submit to the IRS.
- **Security controls:** Computer security controls for systems that process FTI.
- **Incident response and reviews:** Reporting of unauthorized disclosures and IRS safeguard reviews.
