# Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act (HIPAA)

The Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act of 1996 (HIPAA) is a U.S. federal law. Its
Administrative Simplification rules, issued by the Department of Health and Human Services (HHS),
set national standards for the privacy and security of protected health information.

## What it includes

- **Privacy Rule:** Limits on use and disclosure of protected health information and individual
  rights over that information.
- **Security Rule:** Administrative, physical and technical safeguards for electronic protected
  health information.
- **Breach Notification Rule:** Notice to individuals, HHS and in some cases the media after a breach
  of unsecured protected health information.
- **Enforcement Rule:** Compliance, investigation and civil money penalty procedures.
