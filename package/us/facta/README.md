# Fair and Accurate Credit Transactions Act (FACTA) / Fair Credit Reporting Act (FCRA)

The Fair Credit Reporting Act (FCRA) is the U.S. federal law governing consumer reports and consumer
reporting agencies. The Fair and Accurate Credit Transactions Act of 2003 (FACTA) amended the FCRA, adding
identity theft and consumer protection provisions.

## What it includes

- **Consumer reports:** permissible purposes, accuracy and dispute rights
- **Free credit reports:** consumers' right to free annual credit reports
- **Identity theft protections:** fraud alerts, card number truncation and red flags requirements
- **Disposal of consumer information:** requirements for secure disposal of consumer report data
