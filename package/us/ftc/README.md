# Federal Trade Commission (FTC) Act

The Federal Trade Commission Act is the U.S. federal law that created the Federal Trade Commission
(FTC). Section 5 of the Act prohibits unfair or deceptive acts or practices in or affecting
commerce, and is the main basis for the FTC's privacy and data security enforcement.

## What it includes

- **Commission structure:** Establishes the FTC and sets out its powers and procedures.
- **Section 5 (15 U.S.C. 45):** Prohibits unfair methods of competition and unfair or deceptive
  acts or practices.
- **Enforcement:** Lets the FTC investigate, issue orders and seek civil penalties for violations.
