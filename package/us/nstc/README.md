# National Science and Technology Council (NSTC) NSPM-33

National Security Presidential Memorandum 33 (NSPM-33) sets U.S. policy on research security for
federally funded research and development. The National Science and Technology Council (NSTC)
published guidance to help federal agencies implement it.

## What it includes

- **Disclosure requirements:** Disclosure of information such as outside appointments and other
  support by researchers applying for federal funding.
- **Digital persistent identifiers:** Use of persistent identifiers for researchers.
- **Consequences and information sharing:** Handling of disclosure violations and sharing of
  information between agencies.
- **Research security programs:** Requirements for research organizations, including
  cybersecurity, foreign travel security, research security training and export control training.
