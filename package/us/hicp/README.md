# Health Industry Cybersecurity Practices (HICP)

Health Industry Cybersecurity Practices (HICP) is voluntary cybersecurity guidance for healthcare
organizations, produced by the U.S. Department of Health and Human Services (HHS) 405(d) Program
with industry partners under Section 405(d) of the Cybersecurity Act of 2015.

## What it includes

- **Main document:** Describes the most common cybersecurity threats to healthcare and the practices
  that mitigate them.
- **Technical volumes:** Separate technical guidance for small and for medium and large healthcare
  organizations.
- **Resources and templates:** Supporting toolkits and materials for putting the practices in place.
