# DFARS 252.204-7008 to 252.204-7012

These Defense Federal Acquisition Regulation Supplement (DFARS) clauses set cybersecurity requirements for
U.S. Department of Defense contractors that handle covered defense information.

## What it includes

- **252.204-7008:** compliance with safeguarding covered defense information controls
- **252.204-7009:** limits on the use and disclosure of third-party cyber incident information
- **252.204-7012:** safeguarding covered defense information and cyber incident reporting, based on
  NIST SP 800-171
