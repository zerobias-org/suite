# FDA 21 CFR Part 11 (Electronic Records; Electronic Signatures)

This suite covers U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA) requirements, focused on 21 CFR Part 11. Part 11
sets the criteria under which FDA considers electronic records and electronic signatures trustworthy,
reliable and equivalent to paper records and handwritten signatures.

## What it includes

- **Controls for closed and open systems:** validation, audit trails, access controls and record
  protection
- **Signature manifestations:** information that must appear with electronic signatures
- **Signature and record linking:** binding signatures to their records
- **Electronic signature components:** requirements for signature identity and controls
