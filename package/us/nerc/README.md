# NERC Critical Infrastructure Protection (NERC CIP)

The NERC Critical Infrastructure Protection (CIP) standards are mandatory reliability standards for
cyber and physical security of the North American bulk electric system. They are developed by the
North American Electric Reliability Corporation (NERC).

## What it includes

- **Categorization:** Identifying and categorizing bulk electric system cyber systems (CIP-002).
- **Security management and personnel:** Security management controls, personnel and training
  (CIP-003, CIP-004).
- **Technical controls:** Electronic security perimeters, physical security, system security
  management and configuration change management (CIP-005, CIP-006, CIP-007, CIP-010).
- **Response and recovery:** Incident reporting and response planning and recovery plans (CIP-008,
  CIP-009).
- **Information and supply chain:** Information protection and supply chain risk management
  (CIP-011, CIP-013).
