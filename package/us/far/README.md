# FAR 52.204-27 Prohibition on a ByteDance Covered Application

FAR 52.204-27 is a Federal Acquisition Regulation clause that prohibits contractors from having or using
a ByteDance covered application, such as TikTok, on information technology used in performing federal
contracts.

## What it includes

- **Prohibition:** no covered application on IT owned or used by the government or used by
  contractors for the contract
- **Scope:** applies to contractor IT, including equipment provided by contractor employees, used
  for the contract
- **Flow-down:** the clause must be included in subcontracts
