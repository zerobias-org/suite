# FINRA Cybersecurity

The Financial Industry Regulatory Authority (FINRA) oversees U.S. broker-dealers. This suite covers the
cybersecurity expectations FINRA communicates to member firms through its rules, reports and guidance.

## What it includes

- **Applicable rules:** FINRA rules such as Rule 3110 (Supervision) and Rule 4370 (Business
  Continuity Plans)
- **SEC rules:** related SEC requirements such as Regulation S-P and Regulation S-ID
- **Effective practices:** FINRA reports on cybersecurity practices observed at firms
- **Small firm resources:** the Small Firm Cybersecurity Checklist and related guidance
