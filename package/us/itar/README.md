# International Traffic in Arms Regulations (ITAR) - Part 120

The International Traffic in Arms Regulations (ITAR, 22 CFR Parts 120-130) control the export of
defense articles and defense services in the United States. They are administered by the U.S.
Department of State's Directorate of Defense Trade Controls (DDTC). This suite covers Part 120.

## What it includes

- **Part 120:** Purpose of the regulations, definitions and general policies, including what counts
  as a defense article, defense service and export.
- **Related parts:** The U.S. Munitions List (Part 121), registration (Part 122) and licensing and
  enforcement provisions in later parts.
