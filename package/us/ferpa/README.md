# Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act (FERPA)

The Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act (FERPA) is a U.S. federal law that protects the privacy of
student education records. It applies to schools that receive funds from the U.S. Department of
Education, which administers it.

## What it includes

- **Access and review:** parents' and eligible students' right to inspect education records
- **Amendment:** the right to request correction of inaccurate records
- **Consent for disclosure:** written consent before disclosing personally identifiable information,
  with listed exceptions
- **Directory information:** rules for disclosing directory information and opting out
