# SEC Cybersecurity Disclosure Rule

The SEC's final rule on Cybersecurity Risk Management, Strategy, Governance, and Incident Disclosure
requires public companies to disclose material cybersecurity incidents and to describe their cybersecurity
risk management and governance. It amends 17 CFR Parts 229, 232, 239, 240 and 249.

## What it includes

- **Incident disclosure:** reporting material cybersecurity incidents on Form 8-K (Item 1.05)
- **Risk management and strategy:** annual description of processes for managing cybersecurity risk
  (Regulation S-K Item 106)
- **Governance:** annual disclosure of board oversight and management's role
- **Foreign private issuers:** comparable disclosures on Forms 6-K and 20-F
