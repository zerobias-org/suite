# ISO/SAE 21434:2021 – Road vehicles – Cybersecurity engineering

ISO/SAE 21434 is a standard for cybersecurity engineering of electrical and electronic
systems in road vehicles. It is published jointly by ISO and SAE International.

## What it includes

- **Cybersecurity management:** Organizational and project-level cybersecurity management.
- **Continual activities:** Cybersecurity monitoring, event evaluation and vulnerability
  management.
- **Lifecycle phases:** Concept, development, validation, production, operations and
  maintenance, and end of support.
- **Risk assessment:** Threat analysis and risk assessment (TARA) methods.
