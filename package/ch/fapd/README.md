# Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP)

The Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP) is Switzerland's federal data protection law. It governs
the processing of personal data of natural persons by private persons and federal bodies, and is
supervised by the Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC).

## What it includes

- **Processing principles:** Lawfulness, proportionality, purpose limitation, accuracy and data
  security.
- **Controller obligations:** Privacy by design and by default, records of processing activities
  and data protection impact assessments.
- **Breach notification:** Reporting data security breaches to the FDPIC.
- **Data subject rights:** Information, access and data portability rights.
- **Cross-border disclosure:** Conditions for disclosing personal data abroad.
