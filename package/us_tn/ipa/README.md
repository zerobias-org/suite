# Tennessee Information Protection Act (TIPA)

The Tennessee Information Protection Act is a Tennessee state consumer data privacy law, enacted in
2023 as House Bill 1181. It is enforced by the Tennessee Attorney General.

## What it includes

- **Consumer rights:** Rights to confirm, access, correct, delete and port personal information, and
  to opt out of targeted advertising, sale and certain profiling.
- **Controller duties:** Privacy notices, data minimization, security and consent for processing
  sensitive data.
- **Privacy program:** Recognition of privacy programs that conform to the NIST Privacy Framework.
- **Enforcement:** Exclusive enforcement by the Attorney General.
