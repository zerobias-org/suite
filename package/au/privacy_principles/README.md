# Australian Privacy Principles

The Australian Privacy Principles (APPs) are the 13 principles in Schedule 1 of Australia's
Privacy Act 1988. They set the standards for how covered entities handle personal information.

## What it includes

- **Open and transparent management:** Privacy policies, anonymity and pseudonymity.
- **Collection:** Collecting solicited and unsolicited personal information.
- **Use and disclosure:** Use and disclosure, direct marketing, cross-border disclosure and
  government-related identifiers.
- **Integrity:** Quality and security of personal information.
- **Access and correction:** Individuals' rights to access and correct their information.
