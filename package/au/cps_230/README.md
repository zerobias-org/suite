# Prudential Standard CPS 230 Operational Risk Management

CPS 230 is a prudential standard issued by the Australian Prudential Regulation Authority (APRA).
It requires APRA-regulated entities to manage operational risk, maintain critical operations
through disruptions and manage the risks of service providers.

## What it includes

- **Operational risk management:** Board oversight, risk management framework and controls.
- **Business continuity:** Identifying critical operations, setting tolerance levels and
  maintaining a credible business continuity plan.
- **Service provider management:** Managing risks from material service providers.
