# Privacy Act 1988 (Australia)

The Privacy Act 1988 is Australia's federal privacy law. It regulates how Australian Government
agencies and many private sector organizations handle personal information, and is administered by
the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC).

## What it includes

- **Australian Privacy Principles (APPs):** Rules for collecting, using, disclosing, storing and
  correcting personal information.
- **Credit reporting:** Rules for handling consumer credit information.
- **Notifiable Data Breaches scheme:** Obligations to notify affected individuals and the OAIC of
  eligible data breaches.
- **Enforcement:** Complaint handling, investigations and penalties.
