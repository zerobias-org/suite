# Oregon Consumer Privacy Act (SB 619)

The Oregon Consumer Privacy Act, enacted as Senate Bill 619 (2023), is an Oregon state consumer
data privacy law. It is enforced by the Oregon Department of Justice.

## What it includes

- **Consumer rights:** Rights to access, correct, delete and port personal data, to opt out of
  targeted advertising, sale and certain profiling, and to obtain a list of third parties that
  received personal data.
- **Controller duties:** Privacy notices, data minimization, security and consent for processing
  sensitive data.
- **Data protection assessments:** Assessments for processing that presents a heightened risk of
  harm.
