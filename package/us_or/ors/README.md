# ORS 646A (Oregon Consumer Information Protection Act)

ORS Chapter 646A contains Oregon's consumer information protection provisions (ORS 646A.600 to
646A.628), known as the Oregon Consumer Information Protection Act. The law is enforced by the
Oregon Department of Justice.

## What it includes

- **Breach notification:** Notice to affected Oregon consumers, and to the Oregon Department of
  Justice for larger breaches.
- **Reasonable safeguards:** A requirement to maintain reasonable safeguards for personal
  information.
- **Security freeze and identifiers:** Credit report security freezes and limits on the use of
  Social Security numbers.
