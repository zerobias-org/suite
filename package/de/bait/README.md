# Banking Supervisory Requirements for IT (BAIT)

BAIT is a circular (Circular 10/2017 (BA)) issued by BaFin, Germany's Federal Financial
Supervisory Authority. It sets out BaFin's expectations for IT and information security at
banks and financial services institutions. Institutions subject to the EU Digital Operational
Resilience Act (DORA) follow DORA instead.

## What it includes

- **IT strategy and governance:** Requirements for IT strategy, organisation and resources.
- **Information risk and security management:** Managing information risk, information
  security and identity and access management.
- **IT projects and operations:** Application development, IT projects and IT operations.
- **Outsourcing and emergencies:** IT services provided by third parties and IT emergency
  management.
