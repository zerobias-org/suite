# Federal Data Protection Act (BDSG)

The Federal Data Protection Act (Bundesdatenschutzgesetz, BDSG) is German federal law on
personal data. It supplements the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) with national
rules.

## What it includes

- **Common provisions:** Scope, definitions, data protection officers of public bodies and
  the Federal Commissioner for Data Protection.
- **GDPR implementation:** National rules where the GDPR allows them, such as employee data
  and video surveillance.
- **Law enforcement processing:** Rules that implement Directive (EU) 2016/680.
- **Other processing:** Provisions for processing outside the scope of the GDPR and that
  directive.
