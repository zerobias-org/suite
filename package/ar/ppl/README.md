# Personal Data Protection Law No. 25,326 (Argentina)

Law No. 25,326 is Argentina's national law on the protection of personal data. It regulates
personal data held in files, registers and databases, and is enforced by the Agency for Access to
Public Information (AAIP).

## What it includes

- **General principles:** Data quality, consent, information to the data subject, data security
  and confidentiality.
- **Sensitive data:** Additional limits on the collection and processing of sensitive data.
- **Data subject rights:** Rights of access, rectification, updating and deletion.
- **International transfers:** Restrictions on transfers to countries without adequate protection.
- **Supervision and sanctions:** The control authority, registration of databases and penalties.
