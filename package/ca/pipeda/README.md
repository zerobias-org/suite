# Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA)

PIPEDA is Canada's federal private-sector privacy law. It governs how organizations collect, use
and disclose personal information in the course of commercial activities, and is overseen by the
Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.

## What it includes

- **Fair information principles:** Ten principles, including accountability, consent, limiting
  collection, safeguards, openness and individual access.
- **Breach reporting:** Reporting and record-keeping for breaches of security safeguards.
- **Complaints and enforcement:** Complaints to and investigations by the Privacy Commissioner.
- **Electronic documents:** Provisions on the use of electronic documents and signatures.
