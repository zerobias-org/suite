# OSFI Guideline B-13: Technology and Cyber Risk Management

Guideline B-13 is issued by Canada's Office of the Superintendent of Financial Institutions (OSFI).
It sets OSFI's expectations for how federally regulated financial institutions manage technology
and cyber risk.

## What it includes

- **Governance and risk management:** Accountability, strategy and a technology and cyber risk
  management framework.
- **Technology operations and resilience:** Technology architecture, asset management, change
  management, incident management and recovery.
- **Cyber security:** Identifying, defending against, detecting and responding to cyber threats.
