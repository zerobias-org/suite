# ITSP.10.171 Protecting Specified Information in Non-Government of Canada Systems and Organizations

ITSP.10.171 is published by the Canadian Centre for Cyber Security. It is a Canadian version of
NIST SP 800-171 and gives recommended security requirements for protecting the confidentiality of
specified (non-classified) Government of Canada information held in non-government systems and
organizations, for use in contracts and agreements.

## What it includes

- **Security requirement families:** 17 families, including access control, audit and
  accountability, configuration management, and identification and authentication.
- **Operational requirements:** Incident response, maintenance, media protection, personnel
  security and physical protection.
- **Assessment and acquisition:** Risk assessment, security assessment and monitoring, system and
  services acquisition, and supply chain risk management.
- **NIST alignment:** Numbering kept consistent with NIST SP 800-171 for interoperability.
