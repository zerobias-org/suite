# NYDFS Cybersecurity Regulation (23 NYCRR 500) - Second Amendment

23 NYCRR Part 500, Cybersecurity Requirements for Financial Services Companies, is a regulation of
the New York State Department of Financial Services (DFS). It applies to DFS-regulated entities.
This suite tracks the regulation as revised by the Second Amendment adopted in 2023.

## What it includes

- **Cybersecurity program and policies:** A risk-based cybersecurity program and written policies
  approved at a senior level.
- **Governance:** A Chief Information Security Officer and oversight by senior governing bodies.
- **Technical controls:** Risk assessments, access privileges, multi-factor authentication,
  vulnerability management and asset inventory.
- **Incident response and notice:** Incident response and business continuity plans, and notice of
  cybersecurity events to DFS.
- **Certification:** Annual submission of compliance to DFS.
