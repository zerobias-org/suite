# Singapore Personal Data Protection Act 2012 (PDPA)

The Personal Data Protection Act 2012 (PDPA) is Singapore's general data protection law. It governs how
organisations collect, use and disclose personal data and is administered by the Personal Data Protection
Commission (PDPC).

## What it includes

- **Data protection obligations:** consent, purpose limitation, notification, accuracy, protection
  and retention
- **Access and correction:** individuals' rights to access and correct their personal data
- **Transfer limitation:** conditions for transferring personal data outside Singapore
- **Data breach notification:** notifying the PDPC and affected individuals of notifiable breaches
- **Do Not Call provisions:** rules on telemarketing messages to Singapore numbers
