# MAS Technology Risk Management Guidelines

The Technology Risk Management (TRM) Guidelines are issued by the Monetary Authority of Singapore (MAS).
They set out risk management principles and best practices for financial institutions' technology and
cyber risks.

## What it includes

- **Technology risk governance and oversight:** board and senior management responsibilities
- **Technology risk management framework:** identifying, assessing and treating technology risks
- **IT project management, development and service management:** secure delivery and operation of
  systems
- **IT resilience and cyber security operations:** availability, recovery, monitoring and incident
  response
- **Access control, cryptography and data security:** protection of systems and information
