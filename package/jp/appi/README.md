# Act on the Protection of Personal Information

The Act on the Protection of Personal Information (APPI) is Japan's main data protection law. It is
administered by the Personal Information Protection Commission (PPC).

## What it includes

- **Duties of business operators:** Purpose specification, proper acquisition, data security and
  supervision of employees and contractors.
- **Third-party provision and cross-border transfer:** Conditions for giving personal data to third
  parties, including those in foreign countries.
- **Rights of individuals:** Disclosure, correction, suspension of use and deletion requests.
- **Supervision and enforcement:** The PPC's powers and penalties.
