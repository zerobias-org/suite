# NIST SP 800-37 Rev. 2 - Risk Management Framework

NIST Special Publication 800-37 Revision 2 describes the Risk Management Framework (RMF) for
information systems and organizations, a system life cycle approach to managing security and
privacy risk. It is published by the National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST).

## What it includes

- **Prepare:** Organization- and system-level activities that set context and priorities.
- **Categorize and Select:** Categorize the system by impact and select controls (from SP 800-53).
- **Implement and Assess:** Put the controls in place and assess whether they work as intended.
- **Authorize:** A senior official accepts the remaining risk and authorizes the system to operate.
- **Monitor:** Ongoing monitoring of the system and its controls.
