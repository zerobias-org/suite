# NIST SP 800-207 - Zero Trust Architecture

NIST Special Publication 800-207 describes zero trust and zero trust architecture (ZTA), a security model
that removes implicit trust based on network location. It is published by the U.S. National Institute
of Standards and Technology (NIST).

## What it includes

- **Zero trust tenets:** Basic principles such as per-session access and dynamic policy.
- **Logical components:** Policy engine, policy administrator and policy enforcement point.
- **Deployment models and use cases:** Ways to apply ZTA in enterprise environments.
- **Threats and migration:** Threats to ZTA and steps for migrating to it.
