# NIST AI 600-1 - AI RMF Generative AI Profile

NIST AI 600-1 is a profile of the AI Risk Management Framework (AI RMF) for generative AI. It
helps organizations identify and manage risks that are unique to or made worse by generative AI.
It is published by the National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST).

## What it includes

- **Generative AI risks:** Risks such as confabulation, information integrity, information
  security, data privacy and harmful bias.
- **Suggested actions:** Actions to manage those risks, organized by the AI RMF functions
  Govern, Map, Measure and Manage.
