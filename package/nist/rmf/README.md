# NIST AI Risk Management Framework (AI RMF)

The NIST Artificial Intelligence Risk Management Framework (AI RMF, NIST AI 100-1) is voluntary
guidance for managing risks of AI systems and promoting trustworthy AI. It is published by the
National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST).

## What it includes

- **Trustworthy AI characteristics:** For example valid and reliable, safe, secure and resilient,
  accountable and transparent, privacy-enhanced, and fair.
- **AI RMF Core:** Four functions: Govern, Map, Measure and Manage.
- **Profiles:** Use-case and cross-sector profiles, such as the Generative AI Profile (AI 600-1).
- **Playbook:** Suggested actions for each Core outcome.
