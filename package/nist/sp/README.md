# NIST Special Publications (SP)

NIST Special Publications are guidance documents published by the National Institute of
Standards and Technology (NIST). The SP 800 series covers computer security and privacy, and is
widely used for compliance in U.S. federal agencies and beyond.

## What it includes

- **SP 800 series:** Computer security guidance, such as SP 800-53 (security and privacy controls)
  and SP 800-37 (Risk Management Framework).
- **SP 1800 series:** NIST Cybersecurity Practice Guides with example solutions.
- **SP 500 series:** Information technology publications.

## Official resources

- Special Publications: https://csrc.nist.gov/publications/sp
- SP 800 series: https://csrc.nist.gov/publications/sp800
