# NIST SP 800-63-4 - Digital Identity Guidelines

NIST Special Publication 800-63-4 is a set of guidelines for digital identity services, covering
how people prove who they are, sign in and use federated identities. It is published by the
National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST).

## What it includes

- **SP 800-63 (base volume):** Digital identity risk management and choosing assurance levels.
- **SP 800-63A:** Identity proofing and enrollment (Identity Assurance Levels).
- **SP 800-63B:** Authentication and authenticator management (Authenticator Assurance Levels).
- **SP 800-63C:** Federation and assertions (Federation Assurance Levels).
