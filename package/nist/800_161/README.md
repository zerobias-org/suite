# NIST SP 800-161 - Cybersecurity Supply Chain Risk Management Practices

NIST Special Publication 800-161, Cybersecurity Supply Chain Risk Management Practices for Systems and
Organizations, gives guidance on identifying, assessing and responding to cybersecurity risks in the
supply chain. It is published by the U.S. National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST).

## What it includes

- **C-SCRM across the organization:** Integrating supply chain risk management at the enterprise,
  mission and operational levels.
- **C-SCRM controls:** Supply chain controls and guidance based on NIST SP 800-53.
- **Templates and guidance:** Templates for C-SCRM strategy, policies, plans and risk assessments.
