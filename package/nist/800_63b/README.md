# NIST SP 800-63B - Authentication and Authenticator Management

NIST Special Publication 800-63B is the volume of the Digital Identity Guidelines that covers
authentication of users to online services and the management of their authenticators. It is
published by the National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST).

## What it includes

- **Authenticator Assurance Levels:** Requirements for AAL1, AAL2 and AAL3.
- **Authenticator types:** Requirements for passwords, one-time codes, cryptographic and other
  authenticators.
- **Authenticator lifecycle:** Binding, loss, theft, expiry and revocation of authenticators.
- **Session management:** Session handling and reauthentication.
