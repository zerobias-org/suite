# NIST SP 800-213A

NIST Special Publication 800-213A, IoT Device Cybersecurity Guidance for the Federal Government:
IoT Device Cybersecurity Requirement Catalog, lists cybersecurity capabilities that federal agencies
can require of IoT devices. It is published by the U.S. National Institute of Standards and Technology
(NIST) as a companion to SP 800-213.

## What it includes

- **Device cybersecurity capabilities:** Technical capabilities such as device identification,
  configuration, data protection and software update.
- **Non-technical supporting capabilities:** Manufacturer support such as documentation and
  information sharing.
- **Mapping to controls:** Links between the catalog and NIST SP 800-53 controls.
