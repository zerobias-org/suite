# NIST 800-171

NIST Special Publication 800-171, Protecting Controlled Unclassified Information in Nonfederal Systems
and Organizations, sets security requirements for protecting the confidentiality of Controlled
Unclassified Information (CUI) held by nonfederal organizations. It is published by the U.S. National
Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST).

## What it includes

- **Security requirement families:** Requirements grouped into families such as access control,
  audit and accountability, configuration management, and incident response.
- **CUI scope:** Applies to nonfederal systems that process, store or transmit CUI.
- **Assessment companion:** Assessment procedures are published separately in SP 800-171A.
