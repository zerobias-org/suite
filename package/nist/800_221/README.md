# SP 800-221 - Enterprise Impact of Information and Communications Technology Risk

NIST Special Publication 800-221 gives guidance on governing and managing information and communications
technology (ICT) risk as part of an enterprise risk management (ERM) portfolio. It is published by the
U.S. National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST).

## What it includes

- **ICT risk in ERM:** Integrating ICT risk management with enterprise risk management.
- **Risk registers:** Using ICT risk registers to feed enterprise risk reporting.
- **Companion framework:** SP 800-221A gives a framework of ICT risk outcomes.
