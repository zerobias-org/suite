# NIST Privacy Framework

The NIST Privacy Framework is a voluntary tool to help organizations identify and manage privacy
risk when building products and services. It is published by the National Institute of
Standards and Technology (NIST).

## What it includes

- **Core:** Privacy outcomes organized into the functions Identify-P, Govern-P, Control-P,
  Communicate-P and Protect-P.
- **Profiles:** Current and target sets of Core outcomes for an organization.
- **Implementation Tiers:** Describe how an organization manages privacy risk.
