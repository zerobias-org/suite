# NIST SP 800-82 - Guide to Operational Technology (OT) Security

NIST Special Publication 800-82 gives guidance on securing operational technology, including
industrial control systems (ICS) such as SCADA and distributed control systems. Revision 3 is
published by the National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST).

## What it includes

- **OT overview:** Characteristics of OT and ICS environments and how they differ from IT.
- **OT risk management and security program:** Establishing a cybersecurity program for OT.
- **Security architecture:** Defense-in-depth and network segmentation for OT systems.
- **Applying the NIST CSF to OT:** Cybersecurity capabilities mapped to OT.
- **OT overlay:** Tailored SP 800-53 control baselines for OT.
