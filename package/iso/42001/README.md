# ISO/IEC 42001 - Artificial intelligence management system

ISO/IEC 42001 specifies requirements for establishing, implementing, maintaining and continually
improving an artificial intelligence management system (AIMS). It is published jointly by ISO and IEC
for organizations that develop, provide or use AI systems.

## What it includes

- **AIMS requirements:** Context, leadership, planning, support, operation, performance evaluation and
  improvement.
- **AI risk and impact assessment:** Assessing AI risks and the impacts of AI systems on individuals and
  society.
- **Annex A controls:** Reference controls for responsible development and use of AI systems.
- **Implementation guidance:** Guidance on applying the controls.
