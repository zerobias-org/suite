# ISO/IEC 27090 - Addressing security threats and compromises to AI systems

ISO/IEC 27090 provides guidance for organizations that develop or use artificial intelligence (AI)
systems on understanding, detecting and mitigating security threats specific to those systems. It is
published jointly by ISO and IEC.

## What it includes

- **AI-specific threats:** Threats and compromises such as data poisoning and model theft.
- **Life cycle view:** How these threats can affect AI systems throughout their life cycle.
- **Detection and mitigation:** Guidance on detecting and mitigating these threats, complementing
  ISO/IEC 27001 and ISO/IEC 27002.
