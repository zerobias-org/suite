# ISO/IEC 27701 - Privacy information management systems

ISO/IEC 27701 specifies requirements and guidance for a privacy information management system (PIMS).
It is published jointly by ISO and IEC and applies to organizations acting as PII controllers and PII
processors.

## What it includes

- **PIMS requirements:** Requirements for establishing, implementing, maintaining and improving a PIMS.
- **Controls for PII controllers:** Controls such as lawful basis, consent, privacy notices and data
  subject rights.
- **Controls for PII processors:** Controls such as customer instructions, subcontractors and PII
  return or deletion.
- **Mappings:** Relationships to other privacy frameworks and regulations.
