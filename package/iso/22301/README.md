# ISO 22301 - Business continuity management systems

ISO 22301 specifies requirements for a business continuity management system (BCMS) that helps an
organization prepare for, respond to and recover from disruptions. It is published by ISO and
developed by ISO/TC 292 (Security and resilience).

## What it includes

- **Management system requirements:** Context, leadership, planning, support, performance evaluation
  and improvement of the BCMS.
- **Business impact analysis and risk assessment:** Identifying critical activities and the risks of
  disruption to them.
- **Business continuity strategies and plans:** Selecting solutions and documenting response and
  recovery procedures.
- **Exercise programme:** Exercising and testing plans to confirm they work.
