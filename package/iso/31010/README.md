# IEC 31010 - Risk assessment techniques

IEC 31010 gives guidance on selecting and applying techniques for assessing risk. It supports
the risk management guidelines in ISO 31000.

## What it includes

- **Risk assessment concepts:** How risk assessment fits into decision making and the risk management
  process.
- **Selecting techniques:** Criteria for choosing a technique suited to the situation.
- **Catalogue of techniques:** Descriptions of individual techniques for identifying, analysing and
  evaluating risk.
