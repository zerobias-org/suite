# ISO 31000 - Risk management

ISO 31000 provides guidelines for managing risk faced by organizations of any type or size. It is
published by ISO and developed by ISO/TC 262 (Risk management).

## What it includes

- **Principles:** The characteristics of effective risk management.
- **Framework:** Integrating risk management into governance, leadership and organizational
  activities.
- **Process:** Communication and consultation, scope and criteria, risk assessment, risk treatment,
  monitoring and review, and recording and reporting.
