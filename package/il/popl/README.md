# Protection of Privacy Law 5741-1981

The Protection of Privacy Law, 5741-1981, is Israel's main privacy statute. It defines infringement of
privacy and regulates databases of personal information. Only the Hebrew text is official; English
versions are translations.

## What it includes

- **Infringement of privacy:** Defines acts that infringe a person's privacy and the related liability.
- **Protection of privacy in databases:** Rules for databases of personal information and for direct
  mail.
- **Defenses:** Defenses available against claims of privacy infringement.
- **Delivery of information by public bodies:** Limits on public bodies passing on personal
  information.
