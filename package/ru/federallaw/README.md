# Russia Federal Law No. 152-FZ on Personal Data

Federal Law of 27 July 2006 No. 152-FZ "On Personal Data" is Russia's main personal data protection
law. It governs how state bodies, companies and individuals ("operators") process personal data.

## What it includes

- **Principles and conditions of processing:** lawful bases, consent and limits on purpose and scope
- **Special categories:** rules for sensitive and biometric personal data
- **Data subject rights:** access to information about processing and the right to object or seek
  correction
- **Operator obligations:** security measures, notification of the regulator and localisation
  requirements
- **Cross-border transfers:** conditions for transferring personal data abroad
