# Saudi Arabia Essential Cybersecurity Controls (ECC-1:2018)

The Essential Cybersecurity Controls (ECC-1:2018) are the baseline cybersecurity requirements issued by
Saudi Arabia's National Cybersecurity Authority (NCA) for government organizations and national critical
infrastructure operators.

## What it includes

- **Cybersecurity governance:** strategy, policies, roles, risk management and compliance
- **Cybersecurity defense:** asset management, identity and access, network, data and application
  protection
- **Cybersecurity resilience:** cybersecurity aspects of business continuity
- **Third-party and cloud computing cybersecurity:** controls for suppliers and cloud hosting
- **Industrial control systems cybersecurity:** protection of ICS and OT environments
