# Saudi Arabia Operational Technology Cybersecurity Controls (OTCC-1:2022)

The Operational Technology Cybersecurity Controls (OTCC-1:2022) are issued by Saudi Arabia's National
Cybersecurity Authority (NCA). They extend the Essential Cybersecurity Controls with requirements for
industrial control systems and other operational technology.

## What it includes

- **Cybersecurity governance:** OT cybersecurity roles, policies, risk management and review
- **Cybersecurity defense:** asset, access, network, system and data protection for OT environments
- **Cybersecurity resilience:** continuity and recovery of OT operations
- **Third-party and cloud computing cybersecurity:** controls for OT vendors and service providers
