# Saudi Arabia Personal Data Protection Law (PDPL)

The Personal Data Protection Law (PDPL) is Saudi Arabia's general data protection law. It is overseen by
the Saudi Data and Artificial Intelligence Authority (SDAIA) and applies to processing of personal data
of individuals in the Kingdom.

## What it includes

- **Data subject rights:** information, access, correction and destruction of personal data
- **Controller obligations:** lawful processing, consent, purpose limitation, security and breach
  notification
- **Sensitive data:** additional conditions for health, credit and other sensitive data
- **Cross-border transfers:** conditions for transferring personal data outside the Kingdom
- **Enforcement:** supervision by the competent authority and penalties for violations
