# Saudi Arabia Critical Systems Cybersecurity Controls (CSCC-1:2019)

The Critical Systems Cybersecurity Controls (CSCC-1:2019) are issued by Saudi Arabia's National
Cybersecurity Authority (NCA). They extend the Essential Cybersecurity Controls (ECC) with additional
requirements for national critical systems.

## What it includes

- **Cybersecurity governance:** strategy, roles, risk management and periodic review for critical
  systems
- **Cybersecurity defense:** asset, identity and access, system, network and data protection
  controls
- **Cybersecurity resilience:** continuity of cybersecurity during disruptions
- **Third-party and cloud computing cybersecurity:** controls for suppliers and cloud services
