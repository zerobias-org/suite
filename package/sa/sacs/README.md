# Saudi Aramco SACS-002 Third Party Cybersecurity Standard

SACS-002 is the Third Party Cybersecurity Standard published by Saudi Aramco. It sets the cybersecurity
requirements that suppliers, contractors and other third parties must meet to work with the company.

## What it includes

- **General requirements:** baseline cybersecurity controls that apply to all third parties
- **Service-specific requirements:** additional controls depending on the type of service a third
  party provides
- **Compliance:** assessment of third parties against the standard before and during engagements
