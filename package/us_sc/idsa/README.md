# South Carolina Insurance Data Security Act

The South Carolina Insurance Data Security Act is a South Carolina state law, codified in Title 38,
Chapter 99 of the South Carolina Code. It sets data security requirements for insurance licensees
and is administered by the South Carolina Department of Insurance.

## What it includes

- **Information security program:** A written program based on a risk assessment, with oversight
  of third-party service providers.
- **Cybersecurity events:** Investigation of cybersecurity events.
- **Notification:** Notice of cybersecurity events to the Director of the Department of Insurance.
