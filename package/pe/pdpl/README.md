# Peru Personal Data Protection Law (Law No. 29733)

Law No. 29733, the Personal Data Protection Law (Ley de Protección de Datos Personales), is
Peru's main data protection law. It is enforced by the National Authority for Personal Data
Protection (ANPD).

## What it includes

- **Principles:** Rules for lawful processing of personal data, including consent.
- **Data subject rights:** Rights of access, rectification, cancellation and objection.
- **Obligations of data controllers:** Security, confidentiality and cross-border transfers.
- **Enforcement:** Supervision and sanctions by the ANPD.
