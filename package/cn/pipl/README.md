# Personal Information Protection Law of the People's Republic of China (PIPL)

The Personal Information Protection Law (PIPL) is China's comprehensive personal information
protection law. It regulates the processing of personal information of individuals in China,
including some processing carried out outside China.

## What it includes

- **Processing rules:** Legal bases for processing, including consent, and rules for sensitive
  personal information.
- **Cross-border transfers:** Mechanisms for providing personal information outside China, such as
  security assessment, standard contracts and certification.
- **Individual rights:** Rights to know, access, copy, correct and delete personal information.
- **Processor obligations:** Security measures, impact assessments and personal information
  protection officers.
- **Supervision and liability:** Responsible authorities and penalties.
