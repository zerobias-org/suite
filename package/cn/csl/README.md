# Cybersecurity Law of the People's Republic of China

The Cybersecurity Law is a national law of the People's Republic of China. It sets security
obligations for network operators, protects personal information online and creates rules for
critical information infrastructure.

## What it includes

- **Network operation security:** Security obligations for network operators and network
  products and services.
- **Critical information infrastructure:** Additional protection duties, including rules on
  storing data within China.
- **Network information security:** Protection of personal information collected by network
  operators.
- **Monitoring and emergency response:** Cybersecurity monitoring, early warning and incident
  response.
- **Legal liability:** Penalties for violations.
