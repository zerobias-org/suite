# Serbia Law on Personal Data Protection

The Law on Personal Data Protection (Official Gazette of the Republic of Serbia No. 87/18) is Serbia's
general data protection law, adopted by the National Assembly and largely aligned with the EU GDPR.
It is supervised by the Commissioner for Information of Public Importance and Personal Data Protection.

## What it includes

- **Principles and lawful bases:** rules on when and how personal data may be processed
- **Data subject rights:** information, access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and
  objection
- **Controller and processor obligations:** records, security, breach notification and impact
  assessments
- **International transfers:** conditions for transferring personal data outside Serbia
- **Supervision and sanctions:** powers of the Commissioner and penalty provisions
