# Turkey Regulation on Personal Data in the Electronic Communications Sector

The Regulation on the Processing of Personal Data and Protection of Privacy in the Electronic
Communications Sector is issued by Turkey's Information and Communication Technologies Authority (BTK).
It applies to operators in the electronic communications sector and builds on Law No. 6698 on the
Protection of Personal Data.

## What it includes

- **Scope:** personal data obtained by operators while providing electronic communications services
- **Processing rules:** conditions for processing subscriber, traffic and location data
- **Security measures:** obligations to protect personal data and report breaches
- **Transfers abroad:** restrictions on transferring personal data outside Turkey
