# CMS Conditions of Participation: Hospitals

The Conditions of Participation for hospitals are the health and safety requirements, in 42 CFR
Part 482, that hospitals must meet to participate in the Medicare and Medicaid programs. They are
set by the U.S. Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services (CMS).

## What it includes

- **General provisions:** Basis, scope and provisions for specific hospital types.
- **Administration:** Governing body, patients' rights, and quality assessment and performance
  improvement.
- **Basic hospital functions:** Medical staff, nursing, medical records, pharmaceutical,
  laboratory, physical environment, infection control and discharge planning.
- **Optional services:** Requirements for services such as surgical, anesthesia and emergency
  services.
- **Specialty hospitals:** Requirements for psychiatric hospitals and transplant programs.
