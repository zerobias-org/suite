# Personal Information Protection Act 2016 (Bermuda)

Bermuda's main data protection law is the Personal Information Protection Act 2016 (PIPA). It
regulates how organizations use personal information and is overseen by the Office of the Privacy
Commissioner for Bermuda.

## What it includes

- **Conditions for use:** Rules on when organizations may use personal information.
- **Individual rights:** Rights to access, correct and request erasure of personal information.
- **Security and breach notification:** Safeguards for personal information and notification of
  breaches.
- **Overseas transfers:** Conditions for transferring personal information outside Bermuda.
- **Privacy Commissioner:** Oversight, investigation and enforcement powers.
