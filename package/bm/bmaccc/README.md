# Bermuda Monetary Authority Insurance Sector Operational Cyber Risk Management Code of Conduct

This code of conduct is issued by the Bermuda Monetary Authority (BMA). It sets out the BMA's
requirements for how registered insurers manage operational cyber risk, applied in proportion to
the nature, scale and complexity of the insurer.

## What it includes

- **Governance:** Board-level oversight of cyber risk.
- **Cyber risk management programme:** Identifying assets and risks, and detection, protection,
  response and recovery controls.
- **IT operations:** Service, configuration, change, release and incident management.
- **Security practices:** Access management, awareness and training, data classification and
  vulnerability scanning.
- **Resilience and reporting:** Business continuity and disaster recovery, and notifying the BMA
  of cyber reporting events.
