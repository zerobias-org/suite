# AIUC-1 AI Agent Standard

AIUC-1 is a security, safety and reliability standard for AI agents, published by the Artificial
Intelligence Underwriting Company (AIUC). Organizations can be audited and certified against it.

## What it includes

- **Data & Privacy:** Protection against data leakage, IP exposure and unauthorized training on
  customer data.
- **Security:** Defenses against prompt injection, jailbreaks and unauthorized actions.
- **Safety and Reliability:** Testing and oversight to prevent harmful outputs, hallucinations and
  unsafe tool calls.
- **Accountability:** Responsibility assignment, change management, observability and vendor
  assessment.
- **Society:** Prevention of AI-enabled cyber misuse and catastrophic misuse.
