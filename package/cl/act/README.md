# Act No. 19,628 on the Protection of Private Life (Chile)

Law No. 19,628 is Chile's law on the protection of private life. It regulates the processing of
personal data in records and databases by public bodies and private parties.

## What it includes

- **General provisions:** Definitions and the conditions under which personal data may be
  processed, including consent.
- **Data subject rights:** Rights to information, modification, cancellation and blocking of
  personal data.
- **Financial and commercial data:** Rules on communicating economic, financial, banking and
  commercial obligations.
- **Public bodies:** Rules for processing by public bodies.
- **Liability:** Compensation for damage caused by improper processing.
