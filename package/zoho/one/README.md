# Zoho One

Zoho One is a unified suite of integrated business applications from Zoho, with centralized
administration of users, roles and permissions.

## What it includes

- **Business applications:** Zoho apps for areas such as sales and CRM, marketing, customer support,
  finance, human resources, projects and collaboration.
- **Operations and commerce:** Apps for operations and eCommerce.
- **Central administration:** Central management of users, roles and permissions across apps.
